  - Published: 2026-11-03

  - Authors: Robert Andersen, Anders Holm
  - Published: 2026-11-06
  - Abstract:
    ABSTRACT Although mediation analysis is widely used in human resource management to explain how practices shape outcomes, most cross‐sectional approaches rely on the strong assumption of sequential ignorability. We develop a two‐period difference‐in‐differences framework that accommodates time‐invariant unobserved confounders of the mediator and outcome. Rather than requiring independence in levels, identification rests on restrictions on change, including parallel untreated trends and change‐based mediator–outcome exchangeability. The framework clarifies the assumptions needed to identify total and mediator effects as distinct from those required for controlled direct and natural indirect effects. We extend the method to multi‐period staggered designs and provide estimation, bootstrap inference, diagnostics, and an open‐source R package.

    This research establishes the enjoy-able effect .
