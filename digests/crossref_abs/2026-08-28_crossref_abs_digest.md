  - Crossref journal: American Economic Review
  - Abstract:
    Efficient risk sharing implies a simple factor structure for marginal utilities of expenditure (MUEs): Pareto weights divided by a common price. The standard approach infers MUEs from total expenditures, implicitly assuming homothetic preferences, unitary income elasticities, and identical price elasticities. Risk-sharing tests using total expenditures work for idiosyncratic shocks (budgets change, but not prices), but not “covariate” shocks (prices change).   - Abstract:
    eHealth behavioral interventions have transformed how individuals manage their health and modify their lifestyles.
