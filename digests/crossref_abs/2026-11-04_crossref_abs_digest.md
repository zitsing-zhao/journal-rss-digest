  - Abstract:
    ABSTRACT Although extensive research examines the costs and benefits of adopting new disclosure regulations, little addresses the consequences of regulatory inaction. We investigate whether firms in nonadopting countries incur additional information losses, beyond the forgone benefits, when their foreign peers adopt International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS). Nonadopters bear an absolute liquidity cost as foreign peers adopt IFRS, consistent with negative externalities from IFRS and accompanying reforms.
