We develop a pseudo-likelihood theory for rank one matrix estimation problems in the high-dimensional limit. We prove a variational principle for the limiting pseudo-maximum likelihood, which also characterizes the performance of the corresponding pseudo-maximum likelihood estimator. We show that this variational principle is universal and depends only on four parameters determined by the corresponding null model.
