Our analysis shows that unsold television pilots contain more novel content than aired series, and using novel content from these pilots results in greater audience viewership. Broadly, this suggests that early‐stage concepts that do not advance can still inform effective product positioning. For managers and entrepreneurs, the opportunity lies in reusing novel features from these prior efforts and presenting them in ways that make their value recognizable to decision makers. This requires repositioning the idea and explaining why it is more compelling in the current context, how potential risks have been reduced, and what conditions make adoption timely.
