EXPRESS: Evaluating the Impacts of Bundling on a Firm’s Product Demand and Operational Decisions
2026-10-17 | DOI: 10.1177/10591478261489981 | Production and Operations Management
Authors: Shu-Jung Sunny Yang; Yingying Huang; Hsin Lu; Yuan-Hao Chu
Firms use bundles to influence customer demand, but supplying those bundles often requires operational capacity that is shared across the portfolio. Changing the price, composition, or availability of one bundle can therefore trigger two reallocations: customers reallocate expenditure across bundles, and the firm reallocates operational capacity across the demand those bundles generate. We develop a data-driven framework that links these two decisions. Heterogeneous consumers choose discrete bundle quantities subject to a common budget, while the firm jointly chooses bundle prices and quantities subject to bundle-specific and shared-resource requirements. We estimate the demand and supply models from observed discrete decisions using Bayesian Markov Chain Monte Carlo, retaining the integer structure in estimation and counterfactual analysis.
