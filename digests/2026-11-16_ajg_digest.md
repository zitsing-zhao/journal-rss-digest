- [A Psychometric Evaluation of Single‐Item Measures](https://doi.org/10.1111/1748-8583.70071)
  - Authors: Justin A. DeSimone
  - Published: 2026-11-10
  - Abstract:
    ABSTRACT Single‐item measures are increasingly used in human resource management research due to practical constraints such as survey length, anticipated respondent burden, or repeated measurement. Information concerning which psychometric properties single‐item measures can and cannot meaningfully demonstrate is fragmented and incomplete in the HRM literature. This manuscript provides a comprehensive psychometric evaluation of single‐item measures, drawing on classical test theory, item response theory, and generalizability theory. Logic, math, and previous psychometric research inform the assumptions, practical considerations, and tradeoffs involved in assessing the factor structure, reliability, and validity of single‐item measures. This manuscript concludes by offering practical guidance for researchers and practitioners, including a checklist to aid decisions about when the use of single‐item measures is justified.

    We study how bidders adjusted to a switch from second-price to first-price auction rules on a large online advertising exchange.
